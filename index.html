FIELD NOTES / 001

A clock drawn
across the land.

Each labeled line connects places where the Sun sets at the same local clock time on the selected date. Follow a line and you share an evening, even hundreds of miles apart.

Why the lines break

Time zones change the clock, not the Sun. Lines stop at zone boundaries and coastlines. Daylight saving time creates jumps on March 8 and November 1. Most of Arizona stays on standard time; the Navajo Nation observes daylight saving, while Hopi does not.

A modeled horizon

We use Astronomy Engine and a solar-center altitude of −0.833333°, modeling standard refraction over an unobstructed sea-level horizon. Mountains, elevation, and weather can change the sunset you see. Contours are sampled every 0.1° latitude and clipped to land.
