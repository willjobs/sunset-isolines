About this map

I was interested in seeing how the lines marking 5 p.m., 6 p.m., and later sunsets move over the course of the year.

The colors show hourly ranges on the local clock. Drag the date slider or press play to follow the changes, or tap the map for the sunset time at a location. The map covers the contiguous United States in 2026.

Time zones

Sunset might be at 7:40 p.m. on one side of a time-zone boundary and 8:40 p.m. on the other. That makes the border an edge between two color bands. These boundaries can run through states, following rivers or county lines.

Daylight saving time starts on March 8 and ends on November 1 in 2026. Most of Arizona stays on standard time all year. The Navajo Nation observes daylight saving time; the Hopi Reservation does not.

Method

Sunset times are calculated with Astronomy Engine using a solar-center altitude of −0.833333°. The model assumes standard atmospheric refraction and an unobstructed horizon at sea level. Mountains, elevation, and weather can change the sunset you see.

Curves are precomputed on a 271-point north–south grid, roughly 0.1° of latitude. Land and time-zone boundaries are simplified at 0.002° for display.
